Free Tools Every Content Creator Needs (Running Directly in the Browser)

2 September 20263 min read482 words

The Arabic content creator faces small time-stealing tasks every day: counting article words, cleaning text pasted from Word, generating a URL-friendly slug for a new post, or validating a JSON snippet. Specialized tools exist, but most are paid, ad-heavy, or require uploading your files to some random server. Here are the tools I built and actually use — all running in the browser.

Why local browser tools specifically?

The principle my tools platform is built on: all processing happens on your device. The text you paste never leaves your browser — no server sees it, no storage, no tracking. It also means instant speed: no page loads or processing queues, and it even works offline after the first open.

Word counter and text analysis

The writer's core tool: a counter showing words, characters, paragraphs, and estimated reading time. Practical value: SEO articles need real depth, and the reading-time estimate helps you tune your intro length. Reach it directly: word counter.

Text cleaner

Text copied from Word or PDFs carries hidden characters, double spaces, and formatting marks that break on publishing. The cleaner removes them in one click: unified quotes, stripped extra spaces, tidied punctuation. It saves you a painful manual review every time you move text between tools.

Slug generator

Your article title is Arabic but the URL must be Latin and search-friendly. The generator converts titles into clean slugs (dash-separated words) or handles Arabic text with safe encoding. Clean URLs are SEO basics — see my Search Console article for how URLs affect indexing.

JSON formatter and validator

If you touch any APIs or config files, you need a JSON viewer that pinpoints the error by line. The tool formats the tree visually and instantly tells you where the missing comma is — faster than holding your breath at a cryptic error message.

Quick color tools

Convert any color between HEX, RGB, and HSL, with lightness shades — useful for tuning your brand colors across thumbnails, posts, and templates. My brand orange #FF6600 in RGB is rgb(255, 102, 0) — a small detail until you need it ten times in a design day.

Timestamp converter

When dealing with dates in code or databases, you need Unix timestamp ↔ readable date conversion. The tool shows local time instantly and saves you the classic mental arithmetic.

What comes next?

The platform grows by actual usage: a full meta-tags generator, a Schema generator, and a local image compressor are in progress. If you use a tool and cannot find it — email me; user requests set the build order.

Final tip: bookmark the platform (tools.abdallahabas.com). A creator who saves ten minutes daily gains almost an hour weekly.

Summary: do not pay subscriptions for counters and text cleaners. Local browser tools are faster, more private, and keep your data on your device where it belongs.
